DOSwriter is a minimalist distraction-free Android writing environment
inspired by dedicated writing devices and keyboard-centric workflows
such as the Alphasmart Neo and DOS/Unix text editing tools.

It is designed for fast, keyboard-driven writing with a focus on
stability and simplicity. The keyboard workflow is intuitive and easy to
learn. It has tools that address the problem of editing large text files
on small phones and e-inks.
